## Design principles (govern every decision)
1. Equal visual weight for the three pillars.
2. Evidence over adjectives — every claim backed by a number, artifact, or link.
3. Scannable in 30 seconds, rewarding in 5 minutes.
4. Calm confidence, not hype — precise language, generous whitespace, restrained motion.
5. Modular and additive — structure absorbs new projects/certs/awards without a redesign.
6. Global-caliber visual bar (Anthropic/Vercel/Stripe/Linear), not a bootcamp template.
7. Every animation earns its place — reveals information or marks a real moment.
