# Leadership photos

Drop photos into the matching folder — they appear automatically in that
leadership card (no code changes). Folders map to `slug` in
`src/data/leadership.ts`:

- `president/`        President — Junior Enterprise JEEA
- `communication/`    Head of Communication — Injaz Al Maghrib
- `advisor/`          Event Moderator — Students' Association
- `aiesec/`           Marketing & OGX — AIESEC
- `hackathons/`       Hackathons & Competitions
- `clubs/`            Clubs & Mentorship — Apps Club
- `events/`           Events & Company / University Visits
- `sports-culture/`   Sports & Cultural Events
- `social/`           Social Activities

Name images `01.jpg`, `02.jpg`… (sorted alphabetically; 2+ images auto-cycle).
