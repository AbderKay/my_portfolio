02 · Visual language (applies to every palette)

The system that makes it recognisable and coherent — memorable through consistency, not decoration.

Signature glow

Directional aurora light

Soft, low-opacity radial glows in the primary colour — treated as a light source, never neon. Max two per viewport, always from the same angle.

Geometric motif

Registration brackets

Thin 1px corner brackets and tick-marks borrowed from drafting/blueprints frame key cards. A quiet signature — used to frame, never to fill.

Background

Blueprint grid, faded

A near-invisible grid that only appears near light sources (radial mask). The background is a stage, never a subject.

Radius & borders

One hairline, one radius scale

Single 1px --line border everywhere. Radius: 8 (inset) · 12 (cards) · 18 (media) · full (pills). Slightly engineered, never bubbly.

Spacing rhythm

4px base · 8-point scale

Consistent 4/8/12/16/24/32px steps; sections breathe at 96–128px; text measure ~66ch. Whitespace does the luxury work.

Lighting direction

One light, top-right

All glows originate top-right; subtle shadows fall bottom-left. A single consistent light source makes the whole page feel physically real.
